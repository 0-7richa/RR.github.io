01 / ABOUT

A researcher at the intersection of materials and environmental engineering.

My work brings together material synthesis, structural and surface characterization, and performance evaluation. I am particularly interested in understanding how synthesis conditions and material chemistry influence interactions with target pollutants.

Through a combination of experimental design and analytical interpretation, I investigate the relationship between material structure, surface properties, adsorption behaviour, and treatment performance.
